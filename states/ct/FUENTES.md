| **Permiso desde los 16** | «You must be at least 16 years of age.» |

| **Espera para el road test: 120 días con autoescuela, 180 sin ella** | «Applicants who successfully complete driver education … must wait 120 days after the issuance of the learner's permit before taking the road test. All other applicants must wait 180 days…» |
| **Ley de apartarse (Move Over)** | «When any vehicle is pulled over on any part of a highway or road, drivers must: SLOW DOWN (below the posted speed limit) and… Move their vehicle over one lane whenever it is safe to do so.» |
| **Giro a la derecha en rojo** | «If you are turning right on red, you may turn after coming to a full stop and checking to make sure that there is no oncoming traffic and no sign prohibiting the turn on a red light.» |
| **Tolerancia cero para menores** | «Connecticut has a Zero-Tolerance Law for teens drinking and driving. Even one drink impairs a teen's driving…» |
